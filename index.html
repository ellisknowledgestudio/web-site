Questions people ask

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your hosting and web address are bought in your name. If we ever stop working together, everything stays with you.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from getting your answers and photos. Google verification can sometimes add a few days.

What do I need to give you?

Answers to a short questionnaire, a few photos of your work, and around an hour of your time across the whole project.

Do I have to take a monthly plan?

No. The monthly plans are optional. If you do take one, there's no contract and you can stop with a month's notice.

I already have a website. Can you fix it?

Often, yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you what I'd change and give you a fixed price.

Will the AI assistant make things up?

It only answers from the information about your business that I give it. If it doesn't know something, it says so and takes the customer's details so you can call them back. I check real conversations and fill any gaps.

Can I add the AI assistant to my existing website?

Yes. It's a small piece of code that works on most website builders, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and Hostinger. You don't need a new site.

What happens to customers' chat details?

Enquiries are sent to you by email. I'll add a short note to your privacy page explaining the chat assistant, so you stay on the right side of data protection rules.

How do I pay?

Half when we start and half when the site goes live, by bank transfer. Monthly plans are paid monthly.
